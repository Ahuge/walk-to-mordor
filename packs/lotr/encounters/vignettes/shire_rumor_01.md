# Strange Songs

You follow Milo's directions to the old forest path. The trees here are ancient, their branches intertwining overhead like fingers.

And then you hear it — faint, on the wind. Singing. Not in any tongue you know. It rises and falls, beautiful and unsettling.

You stand still for a long time, listening. When it fades, the forest feels emptier than before.

You mark the spot on your map and walk on, a little faster than before.
