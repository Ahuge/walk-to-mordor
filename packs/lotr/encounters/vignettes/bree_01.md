# The Prancing Pony

The warm glow of windows cuts through the evening mist. Inside, the common room buzzes with travelers' tales — a dwarf arguing about the best ale, a hooded figure in the corner nursing a single cup, and Butterbur himself rushing about with a tray held high.

"Welcome! Welcome! Room? Supper? We've got a fine stew on, and the beds are soft as goose-down!"

The air smells of woodsmoke, roast meat, and adventure.
