# Weathertop at Dusk

You climb the ancient stones as the sun bleeds red across the horizon. From the summit, the world spreads out — the Road winding east, the dark line of the forest to the north, and far away, a shadow that might be mountains or might be something else.

The wind up here has teeth. It whistles through the broken crown of the old watchtower, carrying sounds that might be voices.

You don't stay long after dark.
