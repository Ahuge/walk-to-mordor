# The Farmer's Dog

A shaggy, mud-pawed dog trots out from behind a hedgerow and falls into step beside you, tongue lolling, tail thumping against your leg with every third stride. He looks up at you with the uncomplicated joy of a creature who has never once worried about anything.

You scratch behind his ears. He leans into it so hard he nearly topples over.

After a while he stops, barks once — a sharp, happy sound — and dashes back through the hedge. Back to his farm, his bowl, his warm spot by the fire. You walk on, smiling for no particular reason.
