# Pipe-Weed on the Wind

The path crests a low hill, and the breeze shifts. Suddenly the air is sweet with pipe-weed smoke — someone, somewhere nearby, is enjoying a long, slow bowl of Old Toby.

You can't see the smoker. Just the smell, drifting across the fields like a memory of every comfortable evening you've ever known.

For a moment the road doesn't feel so long.
