# Elara

A tall elf with silver hair bound in a single braid. She moves without sound and speaks even less, but when she does, it's worth hearing.

## Backstory

Elara is a warden of the Grey Havens, walking the old roads to ensure they're safe for those who'll follow. She's seen three ages pass and remembers when the trees were young.

## Dialogue

- "The wind carries news, if you know how to listen. Today it speaks of rain."
- "You walk heavily, friend. The earth remembers every step. Tread lightly."
- "I knew a hobbit once. Small, brave, foolish. Like you, in the best ways."
- "Time moves differently for my kind. A century is a season. But this walk with you — I'll remember it."
- "See that bird? It's flown from the sea. It carries salt on its wings."

## Lore Drops

- "These stones were placed by Númenóreans. They marked the safe paths. Most have forgotten."
- "The stars you see tonight are the same ones Elbereth kindled. They've watched over every traveler."

## Farewell

"The road forks here. May the stars shine upon your path, mellon. We will meet again — in this life or the next."
