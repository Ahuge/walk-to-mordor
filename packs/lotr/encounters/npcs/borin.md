# Borin Ironfoot

A stout dwarf with a braided red beard and a laugh like tumbling rocks. He's walking to the Blue Mountains to visit his sister, and he's happy for the company.

## Backstory

Borin was a blacksmith in the Iron Hills until a furnace explosion took two fingers and his nerve. Now he mends pots and pans on the road, telling anyone who'll listen about the old days.

## Dialogue

- "Hah! You walk like my cousin Dwalin after his third ale — all elbows!"
- "See this scar? Troll. Well... a particularly aggressive goat. But it SOUNDS better as troll."
- "My sister makes the best seed-cake this side of the Misty Mountains. You'll see. If we ever get there."
- "Dwarves don't get lost. We take... scenic routes. Very scenic. For years, sometimes."
- "You know what the problem with elves is? Too quiet. How do you know they're not plotting? Hmm?"
- "I once arm-wrestled a bear. Lost. But the bear respects me now. We nod when we pass."

## Lore Drops

- "The road we're on? Old dwarf-road, this. My grandfather helped cut it. Mind the loose stones near the ford."
- "If you ever get to Erebor, ask for Fundin. Tell him Borin sent you. He'll charge you double, but it's the principle."

## Farewell

"Well, this is where I turn off. Safe travels, friend. If you ever need a pot mended... just shout really loud. I'll probably hear you."
