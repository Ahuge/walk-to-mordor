# Pippa Underbough

A cheerful young hobbit with curly brown hair and perpetually muddy feet. She's on her first big adventure and treats everything like a wonder.

## Backstory

Pippa left the Shire with a packed lunch and no plan. She's been walking for three weeks and has eaten her way through half the inns in Eriador. Her mother thinks she's visiting cousins.

## Dialogue

- "Ooh, is that a mushroom? Can we eat it? ...Probably not. But can we?"
- "I've walked SO far today. My feet are going to fall off. ...They're fine. Let's keep going!"
- "Do you think dragons like second breakfast? Asking for a friend."
- "My cousin says walking is just falling forward and catching yourself. He's very wise. He's also six."
- "I packed twelve handkerchiefs. I've used eleven. This is a crisis."

## Lore Drops

- "My gran says the Old Forest used to stretch all the way to the Shire. Imagine! Trees everywhere!"
- "Did you know hobbits invented golf? It's true! Well, my uncle says so."

## Farewell

"Well, I smell pie from that farm over there. PRIORITIES. Thanks for walking with me — you're my favorite tall person!"
