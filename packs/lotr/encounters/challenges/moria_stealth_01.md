# Silent Passage

Ahead, the tunnel widens into a vast cavern. You hear it before you see it — the slow, deep breathing of a cave troll, asleep against the far wall. Its club rests across its knees.

Sneak past. Stay under 1.5 km/h for 300m. One loud step and it wakes.
