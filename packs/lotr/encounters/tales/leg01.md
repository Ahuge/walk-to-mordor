# The Long-Expected Party

In the Shire, in the quiet days before the Shadow fell, there lived a hobbit named Bilbo Baggins. He was a respectable hobbit — never had adventures, never did anything unexpected — until the day a wizard knocked on his door.

You know this story. Everyone knows this story. But sitting here, under these stars, with the road stretching ahead of you, it feels different. It feels like a beginning.

Bilbo left the Shire with nothing but a pocket handkerchief and a sense of unease. He returned with a ring, a sword, and a story that would change the world.

What will you return with?
